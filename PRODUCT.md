# Product truth

## Purpose

The GRC and AI governance engine is a public proof-of-work system. It demonstrates how an enterprise can turn context, telemetry, evidence, policy, and accountable review into repeatable assurance outputs.

## Audience

- CISOs and security engineering directors evaluating the candidate's systems thinking.
- Heads of GRC, privacy, AI governance, and TPRM evaluating audit discipline.
- Hiring managers evaluating code quality, scope control, and executive communication.

## Product promise

Every visible conclusion is traceable to context, a test, an evidence reference, and a decision state. Missing data remains visible. Human judgment remains human.

## Non-claims

The project does not claim a certification, audit opinion, legal conclusion, production customer, or measured business outcome without supporting evidence.

## Public-data boundary

Public deployments use synthetic or sanitized data. Customer evidence, tokens, contracts, and raw security telemetry belong in private storage with access control, retention, and deletion rules.
