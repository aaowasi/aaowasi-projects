Delegation boundary

The agent does the legwork. The accountable reviewer makes the call.

Automation can prepare

  • Evidence request lists, first-pass registers and document cross-checks
  • Gap and readiness drafts, control mappings and questionnaire preparation
  • Vendor and AI-system inventory normalization
  • Change signals, missing-item queues and candidate remediation
  • Draft audit walkthroughs, sampling plans and management summaries

Human decision rights stay explicit

  • Scope, applicability and materiality
  • Whether evidence is sufficient and trustworthy
  • Whether a control is designed and operating effectively
  • Exception, treatment, residual-risk and risk-acceptance decisions
  • Release, authorization, sign-off and accountable escalation

Automation is treated as a preparation layer, not as delegated authority, certification, legal judgment or an audit opinion.

Implementation boundary

What this public portfolio proves—and what it does not.

The public system demonstrates review logic, synthetic scenarios, browser-local records, scoring, evidence states, decision exports and human authorization gates. It does not claim a production autonomous-agent runtime, live PR security scanner, client telemetry, independent certification, legal advice or an audit opinion. Production integrations require authorized data access, security review, tenancy controls, monitoring and an agreed implementation scope.
