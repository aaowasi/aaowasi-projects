# Finished-deliverable commercialization and hiring plan

Updated 9 October 2026 (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur). Prices, timing and funnel targets below are hypotheses for scoped proposals, not past results or guarantees.

## Positioning

**I turn AI, supplier and control evidence into review packs with clear decision prerequisites, accountable actions and an editable handover.**

Buyer entry points are three deliverables; all 29 dedicated domain projects remain available as technical depth. Each has its own operational inputs and output. Shared ownership and evidence provenance connect the work without imposing one risk score on unrelated decisions.

| Buyer | Trigger | Initial deliverable | Acceptance boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI governance lead | A proposed AI use case needs review | AI use-case review pack | Agreed inventory, applicability notes, evaluation gaps and release-review checklist; no safety or compliance certification |
| CISO / procurement lead | Supplier onboarding or renewal | Supplier decision pack | Named supplier population, concentration and data-use review, evidence gaps, owner actions and decision memo; approval remains with the client |
| Audit lead | Evidence requests cannot be reconciled | Control evidence readiness pack | Agreed control/request population, dated coverage, exceptions and treatment register; no external audit opinion |
| Hiring manager | Needs someone who can build and explain GRC workflows | Role-specific demonstration | Show the relevant module, calculation, failing case, source/test and production boundary; explain actual personal contributions |

## Observed benchmarks and inferred actions

Sequoia's Julien Bek published **Services: The New Software** on 5 March 2026: https://sequoiacap.com/article/services-the-new-software . The useful strategic point is purchasing completed work and starting with a bounded task already outsourced. This is a thesis, not proof that this portfolio will generate immediate sales or software margins. The supplied image's market-size and margin numbers are not adopted as financial forecasts.

Vanta's questionnaire product presents evidence-backed preparation with human review/approval: https://www.vanta.com/products/questionnaire-automation . LinkedIn's skills-based hiring guidance supports demonstrating applicable skills: https://business.linkedin.com/hire/resources/talent-acquisition/adopting-skills-based-hiring . Neither source identifies a universally fastest hiring tactic or proves that cold outreach outperforms applications.

**Inference for this suite:** make one review pack understandable and inspectable, demonstrate one real calculation, and show the human acceptance boundary. Pair targeted applications with relevant, individually researched outreach. Keep clients, certifications, employment history and outcomes factual.

## Scope and pricing experiment

Start with a paid diagnostic at the existing proposed USD 750–1,500 range. Quote after confirming one bounded population, source documents, output files, one review iteration and acceptance criteria. Credit a diagnostic toward an implementation only where the proposal explicitly says so. Use the existing COMMERCIALIZATION.md package ranges as internal quoting hypotheses. Do not publish invented scarcity or promised turnaround before inputs are confirmed.

For each engagement record: agreed fee; operator/reviewer hours at internal cost; inference/API costs; hosting; delivery tooling; attributable support and rework. Gross margin = (fee − direct delivery costs) / fee. Utilization and sales overhead are separate from gross margin. Do not target an 80% margin by removing necessary review. Pilot a single repeatable pack before building subscription billing or broad managed infrastructure.

## Daily execution checklist

| Time block | Action | Artifact and completion condition |
|---|---|---|
| 30 minutes | Find active, relevant GRC / AI governance / TPRM roles and buyer triggers | Five qualified targets; record role/trigger, geography eligibility, decision maker, evidence and next action |
| 45 minutes | Tailor two applications | Resume and concise cover note tied to required controls/workflows; apply through requested channels |
| 45 minutes | Prepare three individual outreach messages | One specific trigger, one relevant deliverable, one inspectable link, one low-friction next step; send only after reviewing the recipient and content |
| 30 minutes | Follow up on due conversations | Update the log; one relevant follow-up after 3–5 business days, then respect no response or opt-out |
| 30 minutes | Rehearse one five-minute demonstration | Explain inputs, change a domain value, show the resulting finding/export, and explain approval and backend boundaries |
| 15 minutes | Review funnel and economics | Track qualified visits, replies, interviews, scope requests, proposals and signed work; distinguish observations from targets |

Days 1–2: finalize three sanitized example packs and acceptance templates, tailor the hiring profile and record a concise module walkthrough. Days 3–5: execute the checklist, collect objections and refine the pack description. Days 6–7: review actual results; narrow audience or offer based on evidence, not inflated outbound volume. Repeat weekly. No hiring, revenue or signed-contract date is guaranteed.
