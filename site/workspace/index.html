    Scoring policy & evidence boundaries

    Priority = min(25, max(1, likelihood × impact + signal points − evidence credit)). Unapproved AI adds 3; missing processor DPA adds 3; unresolved processor transfer outside EEA/UK adds 2. Unexpired current evidence with a recorded passing test subtracts 2. High ≥16, moderate ≥9, otherwise low. All thresholds are portfolio policy choices. Scores are ordinal priorities, not probabilities or regulatory determinations.

    Evidence coverage = records marked current ÷ all records. Collection error is separate from failed evidence. A “current” flag is an assertion to verify, not proof of control effectiveness. UK grouping is only a demo simplification; legal transfer and AI classification require manual review.

    AI review is triggered by an AI flag plus missing approval, tested disclosure, tested oversight or a nonempty evaluation set with no failures. The release gate additionally requires unexpired current evidence and a recorded passing control test. Supported answer drafts require unexpired current evidence, a passing control test and a named reviewer, and still require approval before external use.
