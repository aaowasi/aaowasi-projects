GRC suite data

The GRC suite processes records on your device. Save on this device writes records to browser local storage only when selected. Restore loads that saved dataset. Clear records removes it. JSON exports are files you control. Imported records are not sent to a server. Device storage may be cleared by browser settings; retain exports for backup. This workspace provides no account authentication or shared access controls.

Organization evaluations

Public workspace and suite records remain browser-local. Organization evaluations are a separate server-processed service. When enabled, the evaluation form identifies the configured processing provider and requires authorization before sending context.

The service records verified membership, organization usage, a request hash and completed reports for delivery and idempotent retries. Failed requests retain metering status and request hashes; submitted context is sent to the configured provider, whose retention and processing terms must be agreed before organization access is provisioned.

Use sanitized metadata. Do not submit passwords, confidential documents or personal customer records. Contact Abdullah Al Owasi to request access, retention terms or deletion. Billing and ongoing access require separately agreed terms.
