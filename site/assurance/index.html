What the evidence
does—and does not—prove.

Recorded workflow results prove that named repository checks completed at specific commits. They do not prove client outcomes, audit completion, control effectiveness or certification.

The interactive review model keeps collection errors, failed tests, stale evidence and missing evidence distinct so unsupported assurance claims remain visible.
